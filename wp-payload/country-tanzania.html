The TBS certificate, and who handles it

The Tanzania Bureau of Standards (TBS) checks regulated imports before they leave the exporting country. Under its Pre-shipment Verification of Conformity (PVoC) programme, plastic pipes, fittings, hose and valves need a Certificate of Conformity (CoC) issued in India before the goods are shipped.

On an order from us, that is our job. We book the inspection, deal with the inspection company over test reports and pay the fee, and the fee is built into the price we quote you.

  • Who inspects in India. SGS, Intertek and TÜV Rheinland cover the Indian subcontinent under TBS's 2025 to 2028 contracts.
  • Routes and fees. Route A inspects and tests each shipment. Route B is for products registered after earlier shipments, and Route C is open only to manufacturers. TBS sets the fee at 0.50% of FOB value on Route A, 0.45% on Route B and 0.25% on Route C, with a minimum of USD 250 and a maximum of USD 5,000. We pay it.
  • Without a CoC. Regulated goods that arrive without one go to destination inspection and pay a penalty of 15% of their invoice value, as assessed by the Tanzania Revenue Authority (TRA), plus inspection and testing fees. Goods that fail are returned or destroyed at the importer's expense.
  • At your end. A CoC is valid for three months from issue. Before clearance, you or your agent submit the original to TBS through its online application system for authentication, with the bill of lading, the commercial invoice and the TRA assessment.

Certification runs against Tanzania Standards or accepted international ones: TZS 2344 (ISO 15874) for PP-R, TZS 2674 (ISO 4427) for PE water pipe and TZS 233 (IEC 61386) for conduit. Our pipe and conduit are made to Indian Standards, so before we load, we agree with the inspection company which test reports it accepts against each one.

Paperwork at Dar es Salaam

Your clearing agent lodges the declaration in TANCIS, TRA's customs system, with the final invoice, the packing list, the bill of lading and the import permits for your goods, which for these lines means the authenticated TBS CoC. TRA asks agents to lodge at least seven days before the vessel arrives, so pass the documents to your agent as soon as you have them.

On our side, we prepare the export documents. Our export documents pack note covers each one, and our HS codes guide gives the first six digits for each item, which your agent extends to the full tariff line.

Questions Tanzanian buyers ask

Who arranges the TBS PVoC certificate on an order from you?

We do. We book the inspection with SGS, Intertek or TÜV Rheinland in India, settle the test reports with them and pay the fee, which is built into the price we quote. You or your agent then submit the original CoC to TBS for authentication before clearance.

What is a HDPE pipe used for?

Water mains, irrigation, borehole delivery and industrial water lines, because it is flexible, does not corrode and joins by fusion. Double-wall corrugated HDPE duct is also used to protect underground power and telecom cables.

Which pipe is better, HDPE or PVC?

HDPE is better where the pipe is buried, long or on moving ground, because it bends instead of cracking. PVC is better for straight, accessible cold-water and drainage runs, and it costs less per metre. Many projects use both: HDPE for the main, uPVC or PPR inside the building. Our guide to which pipe for which job has the temperature and pressure limits.

What are the sizes of uPVC pipes in millimetres and inches?

Metric uPVC pressure pipe is named by outside diameter: 20, 25, 32, 40, 50, 63, 75, 90, 110 mm and up. Inch-system Schedule 40 pipe uses different outside diameters: ½″ is 21.3 mm, 1″ is 33.4 mm, 2″ is 60.3 mm and 4″ is 114.3 mm, and the two systems do not share fittings. Local price lists often add a letter, Class B, C or D, taken from old British Standards for uPVC pipe (BS 3505 and BS 3506, both withdrawn in 2022), where B is 6 bar, C is 9 bar and D is 12 bar. Compare pipes by pressure in bar. The full table is in our PVC pipe size chart.

Which brand of HDPE pipe is best?

Look for the pipe whose print legend proves what you are buying: the material grade (PE 100 or PE 80), the pressure rating (PN) or SDR, the standard, the size and a batch code. Two pipes of the same diameter but different PN ratings are different products.
