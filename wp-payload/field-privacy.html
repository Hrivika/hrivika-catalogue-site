Privacy Policy
Hrivika Field.

Hrivika Field is a private business application published by Hrivika Global. It is used only by authorised Hrivika Global sales staff to record their own visits to prospective business customers. It is not available to the general public and cannot be used without a Hrivika Global account approved in advance.

Hrivika Field is a workplace tool, not a consumer app. It contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking SDKs, and no data is sold or shared with third parties.

01Who this policy is for

This policy covers two groups:

  1. Our staff who use the app.
  2. The businesses they visit — company contact details recorded during a sales visit.

02What the app collects

The app collects the following, and only while a member of staff is actively using it:

DataWhyWhere it goes
Account details (name, email of the signed-in staff member) To confirm the user is an approved Hrivika Global employee and to attribute each visit to the person who recorded it Hrivika Global's Google Workspace
Business contact information (company name, contact person, phone number, WhatsApp number, email address, designation, business address) This is the purpose of the app — recording prospective business customers Hrivika Global's Google Sheets and Google Drive
Commercial notes (products of interest, quantities, suppliers, pricing stage, remarks, voice notes) Sales record-keeping Hrivika Global's Google Sheets and Google Drive
Photographs (a photograph of the staff member at the business premises, photographs of products, photographs of business cards) To verify the visit took place and to record what the business stocks or needs Hrivika Global's Google Drive
Precise location (GPS coordinates at the time a visit is recorded) To record where the visit took place and to show nearby prospects on a map Hrivika Global's Google Sheets
Device contacts — only when the user explicitly taps "Save to phone contacts" To save a business contact to the staff member's own phone Written to the phone only; never transmitted

Location is collected only while the app is open and a visit is being recorded. The app does not track location in the background, and does not collect location when it is closed.

03What the app does not do

  • It contains no advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • It contains no analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • It does not sell or share any data with third parties for advertising, marketing, or any other commercial purpose.
  • It does not collect data from children. It is a workplace tool for employed adults.

04Where the data is stored

All recorded data is stored in Hrivika Global's own Google Workspace account — Google Sheets for the records and Google Drive for photographs and voice notes. Access is restricted to authorised Hrivika Global staff.

Data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections. Visits are held on the staff member's phone until the phone has an internet connection, then uploaded automatically; photographs are removed from the phone approximately seven days after they are uploaded.

05Third-party services

  • Google Sign-In — to verify the staff member's identity.
  • Google Sheets, Google Drive and Google Cloud Functions — to store and receive the records.
  • Google Maps — to display prospect locations on a map.

These are all Google services governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

06Retention

Visit records are retained for as long as Hrivika Global maintains a commercial relationship or prospect record with the business concerned, in line with normal business record-keeping.

07Your rights

If you are a business we have visited and you would like to know what we hold about your company, or would like it corrected or deleted, contact us at the address below and we will action the request.

Staff members may request deletion of their account by contacting their manager.

08Permissions the app asks for, and why

  • Camera — to take the premises photograph, product photographs, and to photograph a business card.
  • Location — to record where a visit took place, and to show nearby prospects. Foreground only.
  • Microphone — to record an optional short spoken note about a visit.
  • Photos — to attach an existing photograph to a visit.
  • Contacts — only when the user chooses to save a business contact to their own phone.
  • Notifications — to remind the staff member of a follow-up or a planned visit.

Every one of these is requested at the moment it is needed, and the app remains usable if a permission is declined.
