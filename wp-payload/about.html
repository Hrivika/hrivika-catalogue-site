The Letter

The timeline is the record. This is the voice.

You have read the years. Here is what they taught the house — written the way we would say it across the desk.

Hrivika Global Private Limited Merchant Export House · India
India

To the importers we serve — and the ones we hope to,

The timeline above is the short version. The long version is forty-five years of contractors walking up to a counter with a list — and the house finding the material, checking it, and standing behind it. That is the whole business model. It has not changed. Only the counter has grown longer.

Those years taught us one thing worth writing down: materials are specified, not sold. A pipe is a pressure class, a standard and a dimension — not a pitch. So the catalogue at hrivika.com/products reads like a specification sheet — 5,355 products across 777 pages and 4 categories — and the desk answers like an engineer's counter, not a showroom.

You will notice the catalogue publishes no prices. That is deliberate — no two consignments are the same.

Your sizes, your quantities, your mix of products, your port — these set the number. So we quote each consignment on WhatsApp, itemised for exactly what you are ordering. A published price list would be wrong for almost everyone; a consignment quote is right for you.

Here is what you can expect from the desk: the full catalogue open to you, with nothing held back for "bigger" buyers; a consignment quote against your list; export documentation handled end to end; mixed containers welcome, so one shipment can carry pipes beside valves beside fittings; and one number — +91 70702 92133 — that answers 24/7 and carries your order from first enquiry to sailing.

Send us your list. We will do what this house has done for forty-five years — find the material, check it, and stand behind it.

With respect, from our desk in India,

For Hrivika Global Private Limited

Hrithik Garg · Narayan Garg · Vartika Agarwal
