What happens after you send.

Six steps between your message and a sealed container. The load bay beside them is one mixed consignment: pipe, valve, hose and bath fitting going into the same box, under one set of documents.

  1. Step 01

    Enquiry

    Your manifest arrives on WhatsApp at the export desk. A person reads it — not a queue.

  2. Step 02

    Specification

    Sizes, schedule or pressure class, material and finish confirmed against the actual range.

  3. Step 03

    Quotation

    Costed for your quantities and your destination port. Nothing generic, nothing published.

  4. Step 04

    Consolidation

    One container, many lines. Pipe beside valve beside bath fitting — mixed loads are normal here.

  5. Step 05

    Documentation

    Export paperwork prepared and handled end to end — invoice, packing list, certificate of origin, bill of lading.

  6. Step 06

    Dispatch

    Loaded, sealed and shipped worldwide — with the desk still on the same thread.
