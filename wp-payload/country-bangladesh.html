BSTI, the Import Policy Order and your paperwork

Bangladesh's standards body is the Bangladesh Standards and Testing Institution (BSTI). Its mandatory certification list, 335 products as of November 2026, includes two of our lines: uPVC pipes and fittings for drinking water (BDS 1878:2015) and PE water pipes (BDS ISO 4427:2023). Sanitary taps (BDS EN 200) are on it too, which may take in bib cocks. CPVC, valves and hoses are not named.

The Import Policy Order 2026–2029 sends only the goods in its Appendix 4 for testing against BSTI standards at import, and pipes, valves and hoses are not among them. There is no pre-shipment inspection for these goods. Before you order uPVC or HDPE for drinking water, ask BSTI or your C&F agent whether your buyers will expect the BSTI mark when you sell it.

  • Your side. An Import Registration Certificate renewed for the current financial year. Payment can be by letter of credit, TT, open account or the other methods the policy allows; for imports by land, the LC must name the land port. Your C&F agent lodges the bill of entry in ASYCUDA World.
  • Our side. What the policy asks of the shipment: a certificate of origin, and the 8-digit HS code on the proforma, the commercial invoice, the bill of lading and the LC. Our export documents pack note covers each document, and our HS codes guide gives the codes.
  • Package marks. The policy wants the country of origin on the packages, and your name, address, e-TIN and BIN on at least 2% of the largest packages. Send your e-TIN and BIN with the order. Some imports are exempt, such as consignments up to USD 5,000 and bulk cargo; your C&F agent will know if yours is one.
  • SAFTA. Bangladesh's SAFTA concession schedules (SROs of 2015 and 2020) list check valves (HS 8481.30), pressure-reducing, oleohydraulic and safety valves (8481.10, 8481.20, 8481.40) and valve parts (8481.90) at 0% with a SAFTA certificate of origin. Gate valves, ball valves and taps (8481.80) and plastic pipes, hose and fittings (3917) get no SAFTA concession. Ask your C&F agent to confirm the current rate for your HS codes before you ask us for a SAFTA certificate.

By land or by sea

We quote both routes. By sea, FOB at the Indian port or CIF Chattogram. By land, by road through Petrapole to Benapole. Tell us your route when you ask for the price list, because it changes the price.

Petrapole–Benapole, about 80 km from Kolkata, is the largest land port in South Asia, and nearly 30% of India–Bangladesh trade by land passes through it. Indian truck traffic there ran at roughly half its normal level in mid-2026, and the crossing closes over festivals (it shut for eight days over Durga Puja in October 2026), so allow time at the border.

By sea, Kolkata or Haldia to Chattogram is a short run under the India–Bangladesh coastal shipping agreement, but direct sailings have been irregular, and a transshipped container can take several weeks. We confirm the routing and the transit time when you book.

Questions Bangladeshi buyers ask

Can you send goods by road through Benapole as well as by sea?

Yes. We quote by road through Petrapole to Benapole, or by sea, FOB at the Indian port or CIF Chattogram. Tell us the route on your enquiry, and make sure the LC names the land port if the goods come by road.

What is the price of a 20-foot 4 inch PVC pipe in Bangladesh?

It depends on the wall. Our 4-inch Schedule 40 pipe is rated 15.5 kgf/cm² and Schedule 80 is rated 22.5, and the thicker wall costs more. A 20-foot length is about 6 metres (6 m is 19.7 ft); ours comes in 3 m and 6 m. Today's price list, by size and schedule, is on WhatsApp: +91 70702 92133.

What is the price of a 1 inch PVC pipe in Bangladesh?

First pin down which 1-inch pipe you mean. A 1-inch Schedule 40 pipe is 33.4 mm outside diameter; a metric 32 mm pressure pipe is close, but it is a different system and the fittings do not swap. Then the wall sets the price: our 1-inch Schedule 80 pipe is rated 44.3 kgf/cm² against 31.6 for Schedule 40.

How much does 100 feet of PVC pipe cost?

100 feet is five 20-foot lengths or ten 10-foot ones, so price it per length plus the joints rather than per foot. The total then moves with diameter and class.

What is the price of 1 kg of HDPE?

HDPE pipe prices follow polymer prices, which is why some sellers quote per kilogram. A per-kg rate only compares fairly at the same pressure class: a PN 10 pipe weighs more per metre than a PN 6 pipe of the same diameter. Ask every seller for the price per metre and the weight per metre at a stated PN.
