NRV valve: what it is and which one to order

An NRV has no handle, so nobody looks at it again after the plumber leaves. Fit it facing the wrong way and no water passes. Buy the wrong type for a vertical pipe and the tank can drain back down the line.

23 Nov 2026 4 min read Hrivika export desk

Most NRV problems start on the order, not on site. "2 inch NRV" tells a supplier the size and nothing else. Two more words, the type and the pipe position, get you the right valve.

What is an NRV?

NRV stands for non-return valve. Water can pass through it one way only. When the water tries to come back, the valve shuts by itself.

A check valve is the same thing. Indian price lists mostly say NRV. American catalogues say check valve. If one supplier quotes NRVs and another quotes check valves, they mean the same product.

You fit one after a pump, so the water in the rising pipe does not run back when the pump stops. A foot valve is an NRV with a strainer, and it goes at the bottom of the pump's suction pipe.

Which type suits your pipe?

NRVs differ in the part that closes them, and that part decides where the valve can be fitted. Check whether your pipe runs level or straight up before you order.

Here is how that works on a real job. A pump on the ground fills a roof tank through a 1 inch pipe that goes straight up. A lift type will not close properly there, because its disc needs a level pipe. The bronze vertical spring type is made for this spot.

For plastic lines, match the valve to the pipe: a uPVC NRV on uPVC, a CPVC NRV on CPVC. The full range is on our check valve page.

Which way does it go?

Look for the arrow on the body. It shows the way the water should flow: away from the pump, or towards the tank you are filling.

Three mistakes cost buyers money:

  • Fitting it backwards. The line is blocked. A threaded valve can be unscrewed and turned. A valve joined with solvent cement has to be cut out and replaced.
  • Using uPVC on hot water. uPVC is for cold lines. On hot lines use CPVC or metal. Our bronze check valves are listed up to 80°C and the forged brass one up to 90°C.
  • Using it as a stop valve. You cannot shut an NRV by hand. Put a ball valve or gate valve next to it for repairs.

Common questions

What is the full form of NRV?

Non-return valve. It lets water flow one way and closes by itself when the water tries to come back.

Is a check valve the same as a non-return valve?

Yes. They are two names for one product.

Can an NRV be fitted on a vertical pipe?

Some can. A vertical spring type or a swing type works when the water goes up. A lift type needs a level pipe.

Should a geyser have a non-return valve?

A storage geyser comes with a safety valve for the cold inlet. Never remove or block it. If the plumber adds an NRV in the cold line, follow the distance in the geyser's manual, or the safety valve will drip (one such manual).

What is the difference between a foot valve and an NRV?

A foot valve is an NRV with a strainer, made for the bottom of a pump's suction pipe. An ordinary NRV goes in the line, usually after the pump.
