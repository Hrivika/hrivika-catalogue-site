CPVC pipe size chart: CTS, Schedule 40/80 and fire-sprinkler sizes in mm and inches

A site orders 20 mm CPVC for a hot-water line and 20 mm CPVC for a sprinkler branch. Both deliveries say 20 mm. Neither takes the other's fittings.

30 Oct 2026 12 min read Hrivika export desk

The plumbing pipe measures 22.2 mm across the outside. The sprinkler pipe measures 26.7 mm. Nothing was mis-supplied.

CPVC is sold in two diameter systems that share the same size names, and the "mm" on a price list is a label, not a measurement. This CPVC pipe size chart gives the outside diameter (OD) and wall thickness for both systems, in millimetres and inches, taken from the standards themselves.

The short answer

Most CPVC plumbing pipe is CTS, or copper tube size. Its outside diameter is the nominal size plus ⅛ inch: ½" is 15.9 mm, ¾" is 22.2 mm, 1" is 28.6 mm, 1¼" is 34.9 mm, 1½" is 41.3 mm and 2" is 54.0 mm. The pressure class never changes the outside. It changes the wall.

SDR is the standard dimension ratio: outside diameter divided by minimum wall. A lower SDR means a thicker wall and a higher pressure rating. Schedule is the older steel-pipe wall series, and its ratio changes with size.

The IS 15778 foreword says its sizes were derived from ASTM F442M and ASTM D2846M. That is why Indian and American CTS pipe of one size share an outside diameter.

The bore is our calculation, not a figure in the standard: nominal OD minus twice the mid-range wall. For SDR 11 it matches a US maker's published average bore to within 0.1 mm.

Two details matter on site. The ½" walls are fixed values: the standard marks them "not a function of SDR", so 15.9 ÷ 11 does not give you the wall at that size. And SDR 11 and SDR 13.5 pipe of one size share an outside diameter, so they take the same fittings. Astral, for one, states that its CTS fittings are made to SDR 11.

SDR 9 is a company standard

SDR 9 is not in IS 15778. Our catalogue lists SDR 9 CPVC pipe in ¾" and 1" only, on the same CTS outside diameters of 22.2 mm and 28.6 mm. By the SDR definition, the minimum wall works out at about 2.5 mm and 3.2 mm. That is arithmetic, not a published table, so ask for the manufacturer's sheet before you design to it.

Class 3 is the odd one. IS 15778 lists it only from 65 to 150 mm, on iron-pipe outside diameters of 73.0 to 168.3 mm. Our catalogue does not list it, and Indian ranges above 50 mm tend to switch to Schedule pipe. Astral's CPVC brochure, for example, lists Schedule 40 and 80 from 65 mm.

Converting CPVC mm sizes to inches

The millimetre size on an Indian CPVC price list is a nominal label, not the outside diameter. So 20 mm CPVC is ¾" nominal, with a 22.2 mm (⅞") outside. And 40 mm CPVC is 1½" nominal, with a 41.3 mm (1⅝") outside.

Is CPVC the same size as PVC?

Only when both are Schedule pipe. Take 2 inch. CTS CPVC is 54.0 mm outside. Schedule 40 or 80 PVC is 60.3 mm. IS 4985 uPVC has no 2 inch size at all; its 50 mm pipe is 50.0 mm outside. PPR to IS 15801 is metric too, so a ¾" CTS pipe at 22.2 mm sits between PPR 20 and 25 and fits neither.

No fitting crosses between these systems. Where CTS CPVC meets another system, change over through a threaded joint: a CPVC male threaded adaptor or female threaded adaptor on the CPVC side. The PVC pipe size chart has the uPVC, SWR and Schedule PVC tables. If the question is which material, not which size, see UPVC vs CPVC for importers.

How to tell CTS from IPS on site

  1. Measure the outside, not the bore. Put a vernier caliper across the pipe. A 1" pipe reading 28.4–28.8 mm is CTS. One reading 33.4 mm is IPS.
  2. Read the print line. IS 15778 requires the maker's name, outside diameter, class and pressure rating, and batch number, at least every 3 m. CTS pipe shows IS 15778, ASTM D2846 or SDR 11. Schedule pipe shows ASTM F441 and SCH 40 or SCH 80. Sprinkler pipe shows ASTM F442 or IS 16088.
  3. Try a fitting dry. From ½" to 2", IPS pipe is 4.5 to 7.3 mm larger across than CTS. A CTS fitting will not start onto it.
  4. Treat colour as a hint only. IS 15778 calls for off-white CTS pipe, and sprinkler pipe is orange. Schedule pipe varies by maker: light grey in some US ranges, tan with a stripe in Astral's.

Each check takes under a minute. Do them before the cement goes on. A solvent-cemented joint does not come apart.

What to put on a CPVC purchase order

  • System and standard: CTS to IS 15778 or ASTM D2846, IPS to ASTM F441, or sprinkler to IS 16088 and ASTM F442
  • SDR or schedule: SDR 11, SDR 13.5, Schedule 40 or Schedule 80
  • Nominal size and OD in mm: "1-inch CTS, 28.6 mm OD" cannot be misread
  • Length: IS 15778 prefers 3, 5 or 6 m; our catalogue lists 3 m and 5 m
  • Quantity in metres, not in pipes
  • Fittings from the same system, joined with CPVC solvent cement, never PVC cement. CTS fittings have their own Indian standard, IS 17546.

Common questions

What pipe size is CPVC?

CPVC comes in two diameter systems. Plumbing CPVC is usually CTS, ½" to 2", with an outside diameter equal to the nominal size plus ⅛ inch. Industrial and sprinkler CPVC uses iron pipe size, the same outside diameter as Schedule PVC and steel.

What size is ¾ CPVC?

In CTS plumbing pipe, ¾" CPVC is 22.2 mm (⅞ inch) outside diameter, sold as 20 mm in India. The wall is 2.0–2.5 mm in SDR 11 and 1.7–2.2 mm in SDR 13.5. In Schedule or sprinkler CPVC, ¾" is 26.7 mm (1.050 inch) outside.

How do you measure CPVC pipe size?

Measure the outside diameter with a vernier caliper and match it to the chart. 15.9, 22.2, 28.6, 34.9, 41.3 and 54.0 mm are CTS ½" to 2". 21.3, 26.7, 33.4, 42.2, 48.3 and 60.3 mm are IPS. Then read the print line for the SDR or schedule.

Is 2 inch CPVC the same size as 2 inch PVC?

Not usually. 2" CTS CPVC is 54.0 mm outside, while 2" Schedule 40 or 80 PVC is 60.3 mm. They match only when both are Schedule (IPS) pipe. Join different systems through threaded adaptors.

What is the diameter of a 40 mm CPVC pipe in inches?

A 40 mm CTS CPVC pipe is 1½" nominal, with an outside diameter of 41.3 mm, or 1⅝ inch (1.625"). The 40 mm is a trade label, not a measurement.

What is the size of a 20 mm CPVC pipe in inches?

20 mm CPVC is ¾" nominal. In CTS plumbing pipe the outside diameter is 22.2 mm, or ⅞ inch. A 20 mm sprinkler pipe is larger, at 26.7 mm, because it uses iron pipe size.
