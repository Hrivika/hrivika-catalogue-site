The KEBS certificate, and who handles it

Kenya checks imports before they leave India. Under the Kenya Bureau of Standards' Pre-Export Verification of Conformity (PVoC) programme, pipe, fittings, valves and hose need a Certificate of Conformity (CoC) issued in India. It has to be issued before shipment. Once the vessel has sailed, it is too late.

You don't have to chase this on an order from us. We book the inspection, deal with the inspection company over test reports and pay the fee, and the fee is built into the price we quote you. From you, we need the details of your IDF, covered below.

  • Who inspects in India. SGS, Intertek and TÜV Rheinland. Bureau Veritas stopped Kenya PVoC work on 1 September 2026.
  • Routes and fees. Route A inspects and tests each consignment. Route B is a faster route for products registered after earlier shipments. KEBS sets the fee at 0.60% of FOB value on Route A and 0.55% on Route B, with a minimum of USD 300 and a maximum of USD 3,500. We pay it.
  • Timing. The inspection company inspects within four working days of a complete request for sea cargo, and issues the CoC within two working days of the final documents and payment. We plan loading around that.
  • Without a CoC. Goods from India that arrive without one go to destination inspection at a fee of 5% of the approved customs value, and goods that fail are re-exported or destroyed at the importer's expense.

Certification runs against Kenya Standards: KS ISO 4427 for PE pipe, KS ISO 15874 for PP-R and KS ISO 1452 for PVC-U. Our pipe is made to Indian Standards, so before we load, we agree with the inspection company which test reports it accepts against each one.

Duty and documents

  • Your side first. Lodge your Import Declaration Form (IDF) on KenTrade's single window early, because the inspection request needs its details. At Mombasa, your clearing agent clears the goods with the CoC and the shipping documents through the same single window.
  • Fees and duty on entry. Kenya charges an Import Declaration Fee of 2.5% and a Railway Development Levy of 2% of the customs value, plus import duty and 16% VAT. Rigid plastic pipe (HS 3917.21 to 3917.29) normally carries the EAC common external tariff of 25%, but until 30 June 2027 Kenya applies 25% or USD 500 a tonne, whichever is higher. Plastic hose (3917.39) is at 35% for the same year. Your clearing agent confirms the tariff line for each item, and our HS codes guide gives the first six digits.
  • Advance Cargo Declaration. Since 3 August 2026, every container bound for a Kenyan port needs an ACD reference number from KRA's portal at loading, and the number has to appear on the bill of lading. We get it before the container is loaded.
  • Certificate of origin. Kenya has required a certificate of origin with every import since July 2025, enforced from October 2025. We send one with each consignment.
  • Export declaration. Under the Finance Act 2026, you as the importer must keep the export declaration from the exporting country for five years and show it to KRA on request. For goods from India that is our shipping bill, and we send you a copy. KRA has yet to issue its rules on the formats it will accept.
  • The rest of the set. Commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading and the CoC. Our export documents pack note covers each one.

From India to Mombasa

New direct services from Nhava Sheva and Mundra to Mombasa started in September 2026, alongside the existing ones. Transit time depends on the service and its port calls, so we confirm the sailing and the transit time when the container is booked. Our FOB vs CIF note explains who books and insures the sea leg.

From Mombasa, containers go on by rail to the inland container depots at Nairobi, Naivasha, Kisumu or Eldoret, or by road. The Standard Gauge Railway runs as far as Nairobi and Naivasha. Kisumu and Eldoret are reached on the metre-gauge line beyond Naivasha. Rail or road is your choice.

Questions Kenyan buyers ask

Who arranges the KEBS PVoC certificate on an order from you?

We do. We book the inspection with SGS, Intertek or TÜV Rheinland in India, settle the test reports with them and pay the inspection fee, which is built into the price we quote. You lodge the IDF on KenTrade, because the inspection request needs its details.

Is HDPE pipe cheaper than PVC?

Usually not per metre: HDPE generally costs more than uPVC of the same size and pressure class. It can still be the cheaper job, because coils mean fewer joints, the pipe flexes with the ground and fused joints need no thrust blocks. Compare the installed cost of the whole run.

What are the different types of HDPE connectors?

There are three main types. Compression fittings tighten onto the pipe by hand or spanner and suit small sizes; ours are rated PN 16. Butt fusion welds two pipe ends together with a heated plate, which suits larger diameters. Electrofusion fittings have a heating coil moulded inside. Clamp saddles and threaded adaptors connect branches, taps and valves.

How many metres is a PPR pipe?

It varies by maker, so check the length on every quote before you compare prices. A price per length means little until you know how long the length is. Ask the desk for the length of the pipe on your quote.

Which is cheaper, PPR or PVC?

uPVC is usually cheaper per metre, but it is a cold-water pipe: run hot water through it and it softens. If the line carries hot water, compare PPR with CPVC instead. PPR joints are heat-fused, so add a fusion tool to the cost if your crew does not own one.

How does a ball valve work?

A ball with a hole through it sits in the valve body. A quarter turn of the lever lines the hole up with the pipe and the valve is open; turn it back and the solid side blocks the flow. Our forged brass ball valves are full bore, so an open valve adds no restriction.

What is the price per metre of hose pipe in Kenya?

It depends on bore, grade and roll length together, so one per-metre figure misleads. A 1-inch garden hose and a 1-inch heavy-duty suction hose are very different products. Our garden hose comes in 30, 50, 60 and 100 m rolls, and suction hose in 3 to 50 m lengths. Today's price list, by bore and roll, is on WhatsApp: +91 70702 92133.
